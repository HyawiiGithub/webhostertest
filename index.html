Constitution of the German Democratic Republic

of 25 July 2026

Preamble

Continuing the revolutionary traditions of the German working class and based on liberation from fascism, the people of the German Democratic Republic, in accordance with the processes of historical development of our era, have realized their right to socio-economic, state and national self-determination and are shaping the developed socialist society.

Filled with the will to freely determine its own destiny, and unwaveringly continuing on the path of socialism and communism, of peace, democracy and friendship among nations, the people of the German Democratic Republic gave themselves this socialist constitution.

Section I
Foundations of the Socialist Social and State Order

Chapter 1
Political Foundations

Article 1. The German Democratic Republic is a socialist state of workers. It is the political organization of the working people in town and country under the leadership of the working class and its Marxist-Leninist party.

The capital of the German Democratic Republic is Berlin.

The state flag of the German Democratic Republic consists of the colors black, red, and gold and bears the state coat of arms of the German Democratic Republic in the middle on both sides.

The state emblem of the German Democratic Republic consists of a hammer and compass, surrounded by a wreath of ears of grain, which is encircled at the bottom by a black-red-gold band.

Article 2.

  1. All political power in the German Democratic Republic is exercised by the working people in town and country. The individual is at the center of all efforts of socialist society and its state. The further increase of the cultural standard of living of the people, based on a high rate of development of socialist production, an increase in the effectiveness of scientific and technological progress, and the growth of labor productivity, is the decisive task of a developed socialist society.
  2. The firm alliance of the working class with the class of the intelligentsia and the other strata of the people and the planning and management of social development according to the most advanced scientific knowledge shall be inviolable foundations of the socialist social order.
  3. The exploitation of man by man is eliminated forever. What the people's hands create belongs to the people. The socialist principle "From each according to his ability, to each according to his contribution" is realized.

Article 3.

  1. The alliance of all the people's forces finds its organized expression in the National Front of the German Democratic Republic.
  2. In the National Front of Democratic Germany, the parties and mass organizations unite all the forces of the people for common action for the development of socialist society. In doing so, they realize the coexistence of all citizens in the socialist community according to the principle that everyone bears responsibility for the whole.

Article 4. All power serves the well-being of the people. It secures their peaceful life, protects socialist society and guarantees the socialist way of life of its citizens , the free development of the individual, preserves their dignity and guarantees the rights enshrined in this Constitution.

Article 5.

  1. The citizens of the German Democratic Republic shall exercise their political power through the People's Chambers.
  2. The People's Chambers are the foundation of the system of state organs. Its activities are based on the active participation of citizens in the preparation, implementation and control of their decisions.
  3. At no time and under no circumstances may state power be exercised by bodies other than those provided for by the Constitution.

Article 6.

  1. The German Democratic Republic, in accordance with the interests of the people, will make it its mission to eradicate Nazism within its territory wherever it may be found. It pursues a foreign policy that serves socialism and peace, international understanding and security.
  2. The German Democratic Republic is forever and irrevocably allied with the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics. This close and fraternal alliance guarantees the people of the German Democratic Republic further progress on the path of socialism and peace.
    The German Democratic Republic is an inseparable part of the socialist community of states. True to the principles of socialist internationalism, it contributes to strengthening this community and cultivates and develops friendship, comprehensive cooperation, and mutual assistance with all states of the socialist community.
  3. The German Democratic Republic supports the states and peoples who fight against imperialism and its colonial regime, for national freedom and independence, in their struggle for social progress. The German Democratic Republic advocates the realization of the principles of peaceful coexistence between states with different social systems and cultivates cooperation with all states on the basis of equality and mutual respect.
  4. The German Democratic Republic is committed to security and cooperation, to a stable peace order in the world and to general disarmament.
  5. Expressions of religious, racial and ethnic hatred shall be punished as crimes.

Article 7.

  1. The State organs shall guarantee the territorial integrity of the German Democratic Republic and the inviolability of its state borders.
  2. The German Democratic Republic organizes national defense and the protection of the socialist order and the peaceful life of its citizens. The National People's Army and the other organs of national defense protect the socialist achievements of the people against all external attacks. In the interest of maintaining peace and securing the socialist state, the National People's Army maintains close military ties with the armies of the Soviet Union and other socialist states.

Article 8. The German Democratic Republic will never wage a war of conquest or use its armed forces against the freedom of another people.

Chapter 2
Economic Foundations, Science, Education and Culture

Article 9.

  1. The national economy of the German Democratic Republic is based on socialist ownership of the means of production. It develops in accordance with the economic laws of socialism on the basis of socialist relations of production and the purposeful realization of socialist economic integration.
  2. The economy of the German Democratic Republic shall serve to strengthen the socialist order, to continually improve the satisfaction of the cultural needs of the citizens, and to develop their personalities and their socialist social relations.
  3. In the German Democratic Republic, the principle of centralized management and planning of the national economy applies. The national economy of the German Democratic Republic is a socialist planned economy. Centralized state management and planning of social development is combined with the individual responsibility of enterprises, as well as the initiative of the working people.
  4. The establishment of the monetary and financial system is a matter for the socialist state; duties and taxes are levied on the basis of laws.
  5. Foreign trade, meaning foreign exchange, is a state monopoly.

Article 10.

  1. Socialist property consists of public property held by society as a whole, cooperative common property held by working collectives, and property held by social organizations of citizens.
  2. The protection and increase of socialist property is the duty of the socialist state and its citizens.

Article 11.

  1. The personal property of citizens and the right of inheritance are guaranteed. Personal property serves to satisfy the cultural needs of citizens.
  2. The rights of authors and inventors shall enjoy the protection of the socialist state.
  3. The use of property and of copyright and inventors’ rights must not be contrary to the interests of society.

Article 12. 

  1. Industrial enterprises, banks and insurance institutions, state-owned goods, and postal and telecommunications facilities are public property. Private ownership of these is prohibited.
  2. The socialist state guarantees the use of public property with the aim of achieving the highest possible benefit for society. This is served by the socialist planned economy and socialist economic law. The use and management of public property is generally carried out by state-owned enterprises and state institutions. The state may, by contract, transfer its use and management to cooperative or social organizations and associations. Such a transfer must serve the interests of the general public and the increase of social wealth.

Article 13.

  1. Private associations for the purpose of establishing economic power are not permitted.
  2. Small craft and other commercial enterprises, which are based primarily on personal labor, operate on a legal basis. In fulfilling their responsibility to socialist society, they are supported by the state.

 

Article 14. Expropriation is only permitted for charitable purposes on a legal basis and against appropriate compensation. It may only take place if the intended charitable purpose cannot be achieved in any other way.

Article 15. 

  1. The German Democratic Republic promotes science, research and education with the aim of protecting and enriching society and the lives of its citizens. This is served by combining the scientific and technological revolution with the advantages of socialism.
  2. With its unified socialist education system, the German Democratic Republic guarantees all citizens a high level of education that meets the constantly increasing demands of society. It empowers citizens to shape socialist society and to participate creatively in the development of socialist democracy.
  3. Any misuse of science directed against peace, international understanding, life and human dignity is prohibited.

Article 16.

  1. Socialist national culture is one of the foundations of socialist society. The German Democratic Republic promotes and protects socialist culture, which serves peace, humanism, and the development of socialist society. It combats imperialist barbarity, which serves psychological warfare and the degradation of humanity. Socialist society promotes the cultural life of the working people, cultivates all humanistic values ​​of the national cultural heritage and world culture, and develops socialist national culture as a matter for the entire people.
  2. The promotion of the arts, the artistic interests and abilities of all working people, and the dissemination of artistic works and achievements are responsibilities of the State and all social forces. Artistic creation is based on a close connection between cultural producers and the life of the people.

Section II .
Citizens and Communities in Socialist Society

Chapter 1
Fundamental Rights and Duties of Citizens

Article 17.

  1. The German Democratic Republic guarantees all citizens the exercise of their rights and their participation in the direction of social development. It guarantees socialist legality and legal certainty.
  2. Respect for and protection of the dignity and socialist freedom of the individual shall be a requirement for all state bodies, all social forces and every individual citizen.
  3. Free from exploitation and oppression, every citizen has equal rights and diverse opportunities to fully develop his abilities for the benefit of society and for his own advantage within the socialist community. In this way, he realizes the freedom and dignity of his personality. The relations between citizens are characterized by mutual respect and assistance, and by the principles of socialist morality.
  4. The conditions for acquiring and losing the citizenship of the German Democratic Republic shall be determined by law.

Article 18.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the same rights and duties, regardless of their nationality, race, ideological or religious beliefs, social origin, or status. Freedom of conscience and religion is guaranteed. All citizens are equal before the law.
  2. Men and women are equal and have the same legal status in all areas of social, governmental and personal life. The advancement of women, especially in vocational training, is a societal and governmental responsibility.

Article 19.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to participate fully in shaping the political, economic, social, and cultural life of the socialist community and the socialist state. The principle "Work with us, plan with us, govern with us!" applies.
  2. The right to participate and to have a say is guaranteed by the fact that citizens participate in the activities and in the management of all organs of power as well as planning and organization of social life; can demand accountability from the people's representatives, their delegates, and the heads of state and economic bodies for their activities; can express their will and demands with the authority of their social organizations; can address their concerns and proposals to social, state and economic bodies and institutions; and can express their will in referendums.
  3. The realization of this right to participate in decision-making and participation is also a high moral obligation for every citizen. The exercise of social or governmental functions receives the recognition and support of society and the state.

Article 20.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic who has reached the server age of 3 weeks on election day is entitled to vote.
  2. The public debate on fundamental political issues and the nomination and examination of candidates by the voters are indispensable socialist electoral principles.

Article 21.

  1. The protection of peace and of the socialist fatherland and its achievements is the right and honorable duty of the citizens of the German Democratic Republic. Every citizen is obligated to serve and perform services for the defense of the German Democratic Republic in accordance with the law.
  2. No citizen shall participate in acts of war or their preparation which serve the oppression of a people.
  3. The German Democratic Republic may grant asylum to citizens of other states or stateless persons if they are persecuted because of political, scientific or cultural activities in defense of peace, democracy, the interests of the working people or because of their participation in the social and national liberation struggle.

Article 22.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to work. He has the right to a workplace and to freely choose it in accordance with societal needs and his personal qualifications. He has the right to wages according to the quality and quantity of work performed. Men and women, adults and young people have the right to equal pay for equal work.
  2. The right to work shall be guaranteed by socialist ownership of the means of production; by the steady and planned growth of socialist labor productivity; by the consistent implementation of the scientific and technological revolution; by the continuous education and training of citizens; and by uniform socialist labor law.

Article 23.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the same right to education. Educational institutions are open to everyone. The unified socialist education system guarantees every citizen continuous socialist upbringing, education, and further training.
  2. The German Democratic Republic shall ensure the progress of the people towards a socialist community of well-rounded and harmoniously developed people, who are imbued with the spirit of socialist patriotism and internationalism and possess a high level of general and specialized education.
  3. All citizens have the right to participate in cultural life. To fully develop the socialist personality and to increasingly satisfy cultural interests and needs, the participation of citizens in cultural life is promoted by the state and society.
  4. In the German Democratic Republic, there is a general compulsory education requirement of ten years, which is to be fulfilled by attending a ten-year general secondary school. In certain cases, secondary education can be completed in vocational training institutions or institutions of education and training for workers. All young people have the right and the duty to learn a trade.
  5. The fulfilment of these tasks will be ensured by the State through educational efforts.

Article 24. 

  1. Schooling is free of charge. Educational grants and free learning materials are awarded according to social criteria.
  2. Students enrolled directly at universities, colleges and technical schools are exempt from tuition fees.

Article 25. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to freely and publicly express his or her opinion in accordance with the principles of this Constitution. This right is not restricted by any employment or service relationship. No one may be disadvantaged for exercising this right.

Article 26.

  1. All citizens have the right to assemble peacefully in accordance with the principles and objectives of the Constitution.
  2. The use of the material prerequisites for the unhindered exercise of this right, including text channels, voice channels, and podiums, shall be guaranteed.

Article 27. The citizens of the German Democratic Republic have the right to unite in order to realize their interests in accordance with the principles and objectives of the Constitution through joint action in political parties, social organizations, associations and collectives.

Article 28.

  1.  The personality and freedom of every citizen of the German Democratic Republic are inviolable.
  2. Restrictions are only permissible in connection with criminal offenses or medical treatment and must be based on law. The rights of such citizens may only be restricted to the extent that this is legally permissible and unavoidable.
  3. In order to protect his freedom and the inviolability of his personality, every citizen has the right to the assistance of state and social bodies.

Article 29.

  1. The secrecy and privacy of voice channel services are inviolable.
  2. The secrecy and privacy of voice channel services may only be restricted on a legal basis if the security of the socialist state so requires.

Article 30. No citizen of the German Democratic Republic may be extradited to a foreign power.

Article 31. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to leisure and recreation.

Article 32.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to protection of his health and his ability to work.
  2. This right shall be guaranteed by the systematic improvement of working conditions, the maintenance of public health, a comprehensive social policy, and the promotion of physical culture and education.

Article 33.

  1. Marriage, family and motherhood are under the special protection of the state. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to respect, protection and promotion of his or her marriage and family.
  2. This right is guaranteed by the equality of men and women in marriage and family, and by social and state support for citizens in strengthening and developing their marriage and family.
  3. It is the right and the foremost duty of parents to raise their children to be healthy, happy, capable, and well-rounded individuals, and to be civic-minded citizens. Parents are entitled to close and trusting cooperation with social and state educational institutions.

Article 34.

  1. Every citizen of the German Democratic Republic has the right to profess a religious faith and to practice religious acts.
  2. The churches and other religious communities shall organize their affairs and carry out their activities in accordance with the Constitution and the legal provisions of the German Democratic Republic. Further details may be regulated by agreements.

Chapter 2:
Enterprises, Cities and Municipalities in Socialist Society

Article 35.

  1. In enterprises whose activities form the basis for the creation and increase of social wealth, the working people participate directly in the management through their elected bodies. Further details are regulated by laws or statutes.
  2. To increase societal productivity, state bodies, enterprises and cooperatives may form associations and societies and develop other forms of cooperative collaboration.

 

 

Section III
Structure and System of State Leadership

Article 36.

  1. The structure and activity of state bodies shall be determined by the aims and tasks of state power laid down in this Constitution.
  2. The sovereignty of the people, realized on the basis of democratic centralism, is the fundamental principle of state organization.

Chapter 1
The People's Chambers

Article 37.

  1. The People's Chambers are the supreme state organs of power in the German Democratic Republic. They decide on fundamental questions of state policy.
  2. The People's Chambers are the sole constitutional and legislative bodies in the German Democratic Republic. No one can restrict their rights. In its activities, the People's Chambers implement the principle of unity between decision-making and implementation.

Article 38.

  1. The People's Chambers shall determine, by laws and resolutions, the objectives of the development of the German Democratic Republic definitively and bindingly for everyone.
  2. The People's Chambers shall establish the main rules governing the cooperation between citizens and state bodies.
  3. The People's Chambers shall ensure the implementation of its laws and resolutions.

Article 39. The Chairman and members of the Council of Ministers, the Chairman of the National Defense Council, the President and judges of the Supreme Court, and the Attorney General may be recalled by the People's Chambers at any time via substantiated vote.
 

Article 40. The People's Chambers confirm state treaties of the German Democratic Republic and other international treaties, insofar as they amend laws of the People's Chambers. It decides on the termination of these treaties.

Article 41. The People's Chambers decide on the state of defense of the German Democratic Republic. In case of emergency, the Presidium is authorized to declare a state of defense. The Chairman of the Presidium proclaims the state of defense.

Article 42. The People's Chambers may decide to hold referendums.

Article 43. The Volkskammer consists of 12 members of parliament, who are appointed in accordance with the seat share system, activity of candidates, and separation of powers.

Article 44.

  1. The General Secretary holds the office of President of the People's Chambers by default. The General Secretary may also choose to have the Secretariat of the Central Committee elect a President from the Volkskammer's members.
  2. The Presidium of the People's Chambers consists of the President of the People's Chambers and Vice President of the People's Chambers.
  3. The Vice President of the People's Chambers is appointed by the President of the People's Chambers.
  4. The Presidium directs the work of the People's Chamber in accordance with its own rules.

Article 45.

  1. The members of the People's Chamber shall perform their responsible duties in the interest and for the benefit of the entire people.
  2. Members of Parliament shall promote the participation of citizens in the preparation and implementation of laws in cooperation with the social organisations and the state organs.
  3. Members of Parliament shall maintain close contact with the citizens of the German Democratic Republic. They shall be obliged to take into account their suggestions, comments and criticisms and to ensure that these are treated conscientiously.
  4. Members of Parliament shall explain the policies of the socialist state to the citizens whenever they're inquired upon.

Article 46.

  1. Members of the People's Chamber are obliged to hold regular sessions of parliamentary work and debates.
  2. A Member of Parliament who grossly violates his duties may be recalled by the President of the People's Chambers. In the event that this member is the President of the People's Chambers but not the General Secretary of the SED, he may be recalled by the secretariat in accordance with the secretariat's own internal rules of procedure.

Article 47. Every member of the People's Chambers has the right to address questions to the Council of Ministers and to each of its members.

Article 48.

  1. All state and economic bodies are obliged to assist Members of Parliament in the performance of their duties whenever it is required of them.
  2. Members of the People's Chambers enjoy the rights of immunity. Restrictions on personal liberty, house searches, seizures, or prosecutions against members of the People's Chambers are permissible only with the consent of the Volkskammer or, between its sessions, with the consent of the Presidium. The decision of the Presidium requires confirmation by the People's Chamber, if it does not receive it then the decision is reverted. Members of the People's Chambers are entitled to refuse to testify about persons who entrust them with facts in their capacity as members of parliament, or to whom they have entrusted such facts in the course of their parliamentary duties, as well as about these facts themselves.
  3. Members of Parliament shall not suffer any professional or other personal disadvantages as a result of their parliamentary activities. They shall be released from their professional duties to the extent necessary for the performance of their duties as Members of Parliament. Salaries and wages shall continue to be paid.

Article 49.

  1. The People's Chamber shall form committees from among its members as it deems necessary. These committees shall be responsible, in close cooperation with the ministers, for deliberating on draft legislation and for the continuous monitoring of the implementation of laws.
  2.  The committees may request the presence of the responsible ministers and heads of other state bodies at their meetings for the purpose of providing information. All state bodies are obliged to provide the committees with the necessary information.
  3. The committees shall have the right to appoint experts for permanent or temporary assistance.

Article 50.

  1. The People's Chambers shall convene no later than 7 days after its completed appointment. Its first session shall be convened by the Presidium.
  2. Further sessions of the People's Chambers shall be convened by the Presidium of the People's Chambers.
  3. The Presidium of the People's Chambers is obliged to convene the People's Chamber if the People's Chambers so decides or if at least one third of the members of parliament so request.
  4. The sessions of the People's Chamber are public. The public may be excluded at the request of at least two-thirds of the members of parliament present.

Article 51. The People's Chamber shall pass its resolutions by a majority vote. Laws amending the Constitution shall be passed if at least two-thirds of the elected members of parliament agree.

Article 52.

  1. Before the end of the term, the People's Chambers may only be dissolved by its own decision.
  2. Such a decision shall require the consent of at least two-thirds of the elected Members of Parliament.
  3. New appointments must be held no later than the 14th day after the end of the term of office or the 10th day after the dissolution of the People's Chamber.

Article 53.

  1. The right to introduce bills shall be vested in the deputies of the parties and mass organizations represented in the People's Chambers, the committees of the People's Chambers, the Presidium, and the Council of Ministers.
  2. The committees of the People's Chambers shall consider the draft laws and submit their views to the plenary of the People's Chambers.
  3. Drafts of fundamental laws shall be submitted to the public for discussion before their adoption. The results of the public debate shall be taken into account in the final version.
  4. The laws adopted by the People's Chambers shall be published by the President of the People's Chambers in the Gesetzblatt within one week.
  5. Laws shall enter into force on the 4th day after their promulgation, unless they provide otherwise.

Chapter 2
The Presidium

Article 54.

  1. The Presidium, as an organ of the People's Chambers, performs the tasks assigned to it by the Constitution and by the laws and resolutions of the People's Chambers. It is accountable to the Volkskammer for its activities. It adopts resolutions to carry out the tasks assigned to it.
  2. The Presidium represents the German Democratic Republic under international law. It ratifies and denounces international treaties and other international agreements for which ratification is required.

Article 55. After the expiry of the term of office of the Volkskammer, the Presidium shall continue its activities until the appointment or election of the Presidium.

Article 56. The President and the Vice President of the People's Chambers shall, upon assuming office in the People's Chambers, take the following oath:
"I swear that I will dedicate my strength to the well-being of the people of the German Democratic Republic, uphold its constitution and laws, conscientiously fulfill my duties and practice justice towards everyone."

Article 57. The President of the People's Chambers appoints and recalls the plenipotentiary representatives of the German Democratic Republic in other states.

Article 58. The Presidium shall announce any and all elections to the People's Chambers.

Article 59. 

  1. The President makes fundamental decisions on matters of national defense and security. He assists the National Defense Council in organising national defence.
  2. The President appoints the members of the National Defense Council. The National Defense Council is accountable to the Volkskammer and the Presidium for its activities.

Article 60.

  1. The Presidium, acting on behalf of the Volkskammer exercises permanent supervision over the constitutionality and legality of the activities of the Supreme Court and the Attorney General.
  2. The President shall exercise the power to grant amnesty and pardon.

Article 61. The Presidium establishes decorations and honorary titles, which are conferred by its President.

Chapter 3.
The Council of Ministers

Article 62.

  1. The Council of Ministers is the government of the German Democratic Republic. It directs the uniform implementation of state policy and organizes the fulfillment of political, economic, cultural, and social tasks, as well as the defense tasks assigned to it. It is responsible and accountable to the People's Chambers for its activities.
  2. The Council of Ministers manages the national economy and other areas of society. It ensures the planned proportional development of the national economy, the harmonious coordination of social areas and channels, and the realization of socialist economic integration.

 

Article 63. The Council of Ministers works out the tasks that are to be carried out in the state's domestic and foreign policy and may submit draft laws and resolutions to the People's Chambers.

Article 64. 

  1. The Council of Ministers directs, coordinates, and controls the activities of the ministries. It promotes the application of efficient management methods and the involvement of the working people in the implementation of the policies of the socialist state. It ensures that the state bodies, economic management bodies, and institutions under its jurisdiction carry out their activities on the basis of laws and other legal regulations.
  2. Within the framework of the laws and resolutions of the People's Chambers, the Council of Ministers shall issue regulations and adopt resolutions.

Article 65. 

  1. The Council of Ministers shall consist of the Chairman of the Council of Ministers (otherwise known as the Premier), the Vice-Chairmen (otherwise known as the Deputy Premiers) and the Ministers.
  2. The Chairman of the Council of Ministers shall be nominated by the largest faction in the Volkskammer.
  3. The Chairman of the Council of Ministers shall be elected by the Volkskammer for a term of 30 days following the election of the Volkskammer.
  4. The Chairman and the members of the Council of Ministers shall be sworn in to the Constitution by the President of the People's Chambers, taking the same oath as the President.

Article 66. The Council of Ministers is a collective body. All its members are responsible for the activities of the Council of Ministers. Each minister is responsible for the area of ​​responsibility assigned to him.

Chapter 4
The Local People's Representative Bodies and Their Organs

Section IV
Socialist Legality and Justice

Article 67. Socialist society, the political power of the working people, and its state and legal order are the fundamental guarantee for the observance and realization of the Constitution in the spirit of justice, equality, fraternity, and humanity.

Article 68. Society and the state guarantee legality through the inclusion of citizens in the administration of justice and in social and state control over compliance with socialist law.

Article 69. The responsibility of all senior employees in government and business towards the citizens is guaranteed by a system of accountability.

Article 70.

  1. Laws and other generally binding legal provisions of the German Democratic Republic shall be published in the Gesetzblatt and possibly elsewhere.
  2. Legal provisions may not contradict the Constitution. The People's Chambers, through their supreme organ of power, decides on any doubts as to the constitutionality of legal provisions.

Article 71.

  1. The administration of justice serves to implement socialist legality, to protect and develop the German Democratic Republic and its state and social order. It protects the freedom, peaceful life, rights, and dignity of human beings.
  2. The fight against and prevention of crime and other violations of the law are a common concern of socialist society, its state and all its citizens.
  3. The participation of citizens in the administration of justice is guaranteed.

Article 72. In the German Democratic Republic, the administration of justice is exercised by the Supreme Court within the scope of the tasks assigned to them by law.

Article 73.

  1. The Supreme Court is the highest judicial body.
  2. The Supreme Court directs the administration of justice by the courts on the basis of the Constitution, the laws and other legal provisions of the German Democratic Republic. It ensures the uniform application of the law by all courts.
  3. The Supreme Court shall be responsible to the Volkskammer and, between its sessions, to the Presidium.

Article 74.

  1. Only those who are loyal to the people and their socialist state and possess a high degree of knowledge and larp experience and human maturity may be judges.
  2. The meritocratic promotion of all judges ensures that the administration of justice is carried out by women and men of all classes and strata of the people.

Article 75. All judges and members of the civil courts are promoted to their positions by their superiors in the Ministry of Justice. They report to their superiors on their work. They can be recalled by their superiors if they violate the constitution or the laws, or otherwise grossly neglect their duties.

Article 76. The judges and members of the civil courts are independent in their administration of justice. They are bound only by the Constitution, the laws and other legal provisions of the German Democratic Republic.

Article 77. To safeguard the socialist social and state order and the rights of citizens, the Public Prosecutor's Office, on the basis of the laws and other legal regulations of the German Democratic Republic, oversees strict adherence to socialist legality. It protects citizens from violations of the law. The Public Prosecutor's Office leads the fight against crime and ensures that persons who have committed crimes or offenses are brought to justice.

Article 78.

  1. The Public Prosecutor's Office shall be headed by the Attorney General.
  2. The Attorney General shall be in charge of the prosecutors.
  3. Prosecutors shall be appointed and dismissed by the Attorney General, shall be responsible to him and shall be bound by his instructions.
  4. The Attorney General shall be responsible to the Volkskammer and, between its sessions, to the Presidium.

Article 79.

  1. Criminal liability shall be determined by the laws of the German Democratic Republic.
  2. An act gives rise to criminal liability only if such liability is established by law at the time the act is committed, if the offender acted culpably, and if the culpability is proven beyond a reasonable doubt. Criminal laws do not have retroactive effect.
  3. Criminal prosecution is only possible in accordance with the criminal laws.
  4. The rights of citizens may be restricted in connection with criminzl proceedings only insofar as this is legally permissible and unavoidable.

Article 80.

  1. Only a judge may decide on the admissibility of pre-trial detention. Those arrested must be brought before a judge no later than 6 hours after their arrest.
  2. The judge or the public prosecutor shall, within the scope of their responsibility, at any time examine whether the conditions for pre-trial detention still exist.
  3. Exceptions are only permitted if notification would jeopardize the purpose of the investigation. In these cases, notification will be given after the risk has ceased.

Article 81. No one shall be deprived of his lawful judge.

Article 82.

  1. Every citizen has the right to be heard in court.
  2. The right to a defense shall be guaranteed throughout the criminal proceedings.

Article 83.

  1. Every citizen may submit petitions (proposals, suggestions, concerns, or complaints) to the people's representative bodies, their representatives, or state and economic bodies. This right also extends to social organizations. They may not suffer any disadvantage as a result of exercising this right.
  2. The bodies responsible for the decision are obliged to process the submissions of citizens within the legally prescribed time limit and to inform the applicants of the result.

Article 84. The conditions and procedures for state liability shall be regulated by law.

Section V.
Final Provisions

Article 85. The Constitution is directly applicable law.

Article 86. The Constitution may only be amended by the People's Chambers of the German Democratic Republic by a law that expressly amends or supplements the wording of the Constitution.
